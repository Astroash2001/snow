  <action>
1. Drag (no CSS drag region; Chromium drag regions swallow clicks): in `MiniIndicator`, orb `pointerdown` captures the pointer and records the start; if total movement stays under 4 px until `pointerup` it is a click (`chrome.click()`); at 4 px or more it is a drag: send `chrome.drag(dx, dy)` with screen deltas, main moves the window with `setPosition`; on `pointerup` send `chrome.dragEnd()` and main persists `{x, y, displayId}` in DIP to `app.getPath('userData')/mini-position.json`. Dragging never opens the dashboard. After any move, main tells the renderer which half of its display the window is on so the row mirrors when on the left. The preload functions `drag`, `dragEnd`, `onCommand`, `sendState` and `setIcon` already exist from plan 01-25; this plan adds their main-process handlers in `windows.ts` and `index.ts`.

2. `position.ts`: pure `clampToWorkArea(saved, displays, primary) -> {x, y}` (saved display missing -> default top-right with 16 px margins; otherwise clamp fully inside that display's `workArea`), applied at startup and on `screen` `display-removed` and `display-metrics-changed`. Vitest `position.test.ts` covers the behaviors.

3. `orb/icon.ts`: `renderOrbIcon(state, size, muted) -> dataUrl` drawing a tiny orb (a few bright nodes and lines from the atlas in the current state hue, a slash when muted) on an OffscreenCanvas. The dashboard renderer sends 16 and 32 px icons through `chrome.setIcon` on every state change; main sets the tray image with `nativeImage.createFromDataURL` and the window icons (D-31: the orb is the app icon, tray icon and avatar; no snowflake). The renderer also sends `chrome.sendState({ stateWord, muted, attention })` on change (`App.tsx`).

4. `tray.ts`: tooltip `Snow: {state word}` plus ` (attention)` when any subsystem is down or restarting; left click opens or focuses the dashboard; context menu in order "Open Snow", "Mute microphone" or "Unmute microphone" (from confirmed state), separator, "Quit Snow". The mini orb `contextmenu` shows a native menu "Open Snow", "Mute microphone"/"Unmute microphone", "Quit Snow". Menu actions go to the dashboard renderer over `chrome:command`, which sends `cmd.mute.set` with `by: "tray"` or `cmd.app.quit` on its WebSocket (`ws.ts` `sendCommand`); if the socket is offline, Quit calls `app.quit()` locally and Mute stays disabled. Quit has no confirmation dialog (UI-SPEC). Unmute by click only (D-10).

5. `index.ts`: `app.requestSingleInstanceLock()`; on `second-instance` open and focus the dashboard; if `--autostart` or `--restarted` is in `process.argv` (passed by plan 01-14's hub), create the dashboard hidden and show only the mini; otherwise show the dashboard. Keep the hide-on-close behavior from plan 01-25; quitting happens only through Quit.

6. `hud/e2e/windows.spec.ts`: launch flags, single instance (launch a second app and assert the first shows the dashboard), drag and click via pointer events dispatched in the mini page, tray and context-menu handlers invoked through a test-only `electronApp.evaluate` hook exposed when `SNOW_HUD_TEST=1`, and the mock hub's `received` list holding `cmd.mute.set` with `by: "tray"` and `cmd.app.quit`.
