## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** wake Snow by voice and talk with it naturally, **so that** I can speak to my PC instead of typing.

After this plan Snow sounds like Snow: a calm, dry, Jarvis-like companion that answers "Hey Snow" instantly, reads the user's tone, picks the emotion for each reply, tells the user in character when it had to switch providers, and stays silent rather than reciting a canned line when no model can answer.

<objective>

Purpose: SPCH-01, SPCH-02 (LLM side), CORE-10 (in-character switch notice); D-01 to D-06, D-09, D-17, D-28, D-35, D-36, D-41.
Output: persona, emotion and acks modules, persona.toml, four brain test modules.
</objective>
