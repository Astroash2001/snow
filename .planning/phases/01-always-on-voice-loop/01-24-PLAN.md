1. Spike inside this task (D-35 requires checking accuracy, speed and licence): read the emotion2vec-web-distill model card. If its licence permits personal use and it runs on onnxruntime CPU, pin it: config `[tone] model_url = "https://huggingface.co/thomashallock/emotion2vec-web-distill/resolve/<commit>/<file>.onnx"`, `model_sha256 = "<hex>"`, `labels = [...]` from the card; download once with stdlib `urllib.request` into the gitignored `models/tone/` and verify SHA-256 before loading (no new dependency). Do not pull the 300M-parameter emotion2vec+ large model (too heavy for the CPU budget, D-39).

2. `tone.py`: `ToneAnalyzer(cfg)` with `analyze(pcm: bytes, text: str, sample_rate=16000) -> Tone`: voiced frames by energy over 30 ms windows; `loudness_dbfs` = RMS over voiced frames; `pitch_hz_median` by autocorrelation over 40 ms voiced windows (numpy, 60-400 Hz); `words_per_min` = word count of `text` / voiced minutes; categories relative to a running median of the last 20 turns kept in memory (loud above median + 6 dB, quiet below median - 8 dB; fast above 170 wpm or median + 25 %, slow below 110 wpm or median - 25 %), with absolute thresholds from config for the first turns; `emotion` = the model's top label when its probability is at least `[tone] min_confidence = 0.4`, else None, with `emotion_confidence`. Config keys under `[tone]`: `model_url`, `model_sha256`, `labels`, `min_confidence`, `loud_offset_db = 6`, `quiet_offset_db = 8`, `fast_wpm = 170`, `slow_wpm = 110`.

3. Pipeline: start `asyncio.to_thread(tone.analyze, pcm, text)` as soon as the final text is known (STT and tone overlap), wait at most 300 ms more, attach the result to `TranscriptFinal.tone` (None on timeout). The brain formats the tag and balances its answer (plan 01-16, D-36).

4. Tests per the behavior block on the committed `tone_calm.wav` and `tone_stressed.wav` fixtures (plan 01-05); tests that need the downloaded model are marked `slow`.
