## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** wake Snow by voice and talk with it naturally, **so that** I can speak to my PC instead of typing.

After this plan Snow keeps replying when a provider fails: it moves down a config-driven chain, never leaks private turns to untrusted providers, and records who answered. Plan 01-12 adds free-tier limit handling on top.
