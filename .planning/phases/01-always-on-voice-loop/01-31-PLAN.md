## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** wake Snow by voice and talk with it naturally, **so that** I can speak to my PC instead of typing.

After this plan Snow's big moments carry real emotion: greetings, jokes and bad news are spoken by Cartesia's expressive voice within a daily budget, and everything falls back to the everyday voice when Cartesia is unavailable.

Planner assumption (UI Considerations, unresolved row): Cartesia's remaining-credit endpoint and its response shape are unconfirmed (research A11). This plan tries the configured endpoint with the real key; if it cannot read a numeric balance, `credits_remaining` stays null and the HUD shows "Unavailable" with "Cartesia did not report a balance." The daily budget does not depend on it (it counts characters locally).
