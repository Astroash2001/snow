The combined impulse is eq_fir convolved with (a unit impulse + the wet impulse). With eq off, eq_fir is the unit impulse. With both off, skip the stage. Apply it with FFT overlap-add: next power of two, the rfft of the impulse cached per FFT size, and a persistent tail of length len(ir)-1 added to each block's head. This works for any block size, so chunk invariance holds. flush emits the remaining tail through the dynamics, with trailing samples below -60 dBFS dropped. Do not use scipy (undeclared): numpy FFT only.
