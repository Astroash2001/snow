Pause first, decide second (design items 1, 3, 4; UAT G-01-5, G-01-15 cause 2). Speech over Snow pauses the reply and holds the rest of it in RAM. A check then runs off the audio thread: local Moonshine words, plus a difflib self-echo filter against what Snow said in the last 10 s. If the check accepts, Snow stops for good. C. Engine (src/snow/voice/turn.py).

New members:
- `pause: Callable[[bool], None]`, default no-op, next to `duck`.
- `check: Callable[[Pcm, int], None]`. Its default accepts at once by calling `self.verdict(episode, True)`, the synchronous fake RESEARCH asks for.
- Thread-safe `verdict(episode: int, ok: bool)`, appended to `_inbox`; widen the inbox item type as needed. `drain()` applies it.

`_listen_over` follows these rules in audio time; there is no wall clock. An episode is the stretch from the first speech chunk heard over Snow's reply (reply pending, no turn open) to its end. Counting is as today: the run grows on VAD speech chunks, and a quiet stretch over `barge_gap_ms` breaks the run but not the episode. Each episode also keeps `_barge_speech`, its summed VAD speech samples.
- R1: the first speech chunk opens an episode and increments `_episode`.
- R2: duck at duck_after_ms, unchanged.
- R3: when the run reaches the pause trigger (`pause_after_ms`, or `barge_in_warmup_min_ms` while `echo_warming()`), the window stage starts once. Outside a reject hold it calls `pause(True)`, and the window starts `echo_tail_ms` later. Inside a reject hold Snow is not paused (it stays ducked) and the window starts at once.
- R4: window chunks are kept in order. When their VAD speech reaches `verify_speech_ms`, and no check has been called for this episode, call `check(concatenated window, episode)`.
- R5: an ok verdict for the current episode with a check pending calls the existing `_barge_in()`.
- R6: a rejecting verdict gives the voice back, ends the episode, and starts the reject hold.
- R7 (exact trigger): on the chunk where the episode's quiet stretch, counted from its last VAD speech chunk, first reaches `quiet_resume_ms`, with no check yet called for this episode:
  - if `_barge_speech` is at least `check_min_speech_ms`, call `check()` exactly once on the whole episode audio. That is the last `_barge_span` samples of `_ring`, which covers the episode by its `duck_release_s + _BARGE_LEAD_S` sizing. Keep the current pause or duck state until R5, R6 or R8.
  - otherwise give the voice back with no check and no hold.
  This is how a one-word "Stop." gets checked: it is over before the pause trigger and echo tail, so R4 cannot fire for it.
- R8: `duck_release_s` from the episode's first speech without a cut gives the voice back. Any later verdict is stale.
- R9: stale verdicts are ignored: wrong episode, no check pending, Snow no longer audible, or a turn open.
- R10: `on_wake` is unchanged; the wake phrase still cuts at once.
- R11: the hold counts down only while Snow is audible, and a "speaking" inbox message clears it.

Each episode calls `check()` at most once (R4 or R7, never both).
